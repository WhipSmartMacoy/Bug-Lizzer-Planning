Plan one week at a time. Put recipes on the days you'll cook them, and the shopping list fills in with just that week's ingredients, in the order you'll need them. Tick what's already at home so nobody buys it twice.

Shopping list for this week

Tap what's in the fridge that needs using. Recipes are ranked by how many of those ingredients they use up, then by how little else you'd need to buy.

Add a recipe

Paste the link to a recipe from NYT Cooking, Serious Eats or any other site. The box saves the title, photo and ingredient names, and links back to the original.

Counts come only from meals you've put on the calendar: "Planned" is every time a recipe using it was scheduled, and "Cooked" is how many of those actually got made. Ingredients near the top are worth keeping stocked. If you keep buying something that rarely gets cooked, it's probably going to waste.

New recipes in the box you haven't planned yet, ranked by how many ingredients they share with what you plan most. They use what you already buy, so less goes to waste.
